Your Journey Begins Here

If you're new to kidney health management or plant-based eating, follow these simple, structured stages to protect your kidneys without feeling overwhelmed.

01
Foundation

Know Your Key Numbers

Get a copy of your latest metabolic panel and urine test. Note your eGFR and check if you have protein in your urine (UACR). These baseline metrics define your nutritional requirements.

  • Request full printouts from your patient portal.
  • Bookmark our Lab Library to decode what creatinine and BUN mean.
02
Sodium Reduction

Settle Your Blood Pressure

High blood pressure is the second leading cause of kidney damage. Reducing sodium reduces pressure in the renal arteries, taking the strain off your nephrons.

  • Eliminate restaurant meals and processed deli meats.
  • Flavor dishes with garlic, fresh lemon, or onion powder instead of table salt.
03
Plant Substitution

Adopt Clean Plant Proteins

Animal proteins produce higher acid loads, forcing the kidneys to work harder to filter wastes. Plant proteins (like legumes and organic tofu) reduce acid load and slow CKD progression.

  • Swap one red meat meal a day for a lentil or grain-based alternative.
  • Ensure vegetable choices fit your potassium budget.
04
Daily Routine

Download and Track Daily

Consistency is key. Use our interactive daily tracker to hold yourself accountable to adequate hydration, kidney-safe plants, and stress reduction.

  • Print out the daily checklist template.
  • Keep it on your refrigerator or bookmark the tab on your mobile device.

My Mission & Science

Kidney health, explained simply - with science, plants, and lived experience

I'm Lena — scientist, mother, and IgA nephropathy patient.

In November 2021, I was told my kidneys were failing: GFR 18%, proteinuria 5.9g, and dialysis was becoming a possibility.

As both a scientist and a patient, I began learning everything I could — about my lab results, treatment, nutrition, lifestyle, and the mental side of living with uncertainty.

Alongside my medical treatment, I focused on a plant-forward diet, daily movement, mental wellbeing, and scientific self-education.

Four years later:

18% → 45% GFR · stable for 3 years
5.9g → 0.2g Proteinuria
9.5 → 13.9 Haemoglobin

I don't claim to have "cured" myself. Medical treatment, time, and many factors played a role. But I learned that kidney health is more than a number on a lab report.

That's why I created Green Kidney Lab — to translate kidney labs, plant-forward nutrition, and everyday habits into clear, practical, science-based information for patients and families.
